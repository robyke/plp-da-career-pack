# Mock Interview Reflection

## Interview Details

**Interviewer:** Data Analytics peer  
**Date:** 19 October 2026  
**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Interview Focus:** Junior Data Analyst role

## What Went Well

The mock interview helped me practise explaining my technical skills and projects clearly. I was comfortable discussing Python, SQL, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis and dashboarding.

I explained my PesaPap capstone well and was able to describe how I cleaned 70,828 raw transactions and produced 70,824 valid transactions. I also explained how I used SQL, pandas, statistical testing, customer segmentation and cohort analysis to generate business insights.

I felt confident when answering questions about LEFT JOINs, missing data, mean versus median and how I would investigate a sudden change in business performance.

Another strength was connecting technical analysis to business decisions. Instead of only describing the code I used, I explained findings such as High Value customers contributing about 68.95% of total transaction value and how this could support retention decisions.

## What I Will Improve

I need to make some of my answers shorter and more direct. During the interview, I sometimes gave more technical detail than necessary before giving the main answer.

I also want to improve how quickly I explain statistical concepts such as p-values in simple business language.

Another area for improvement is speaking more confidently about my recommendations and clearly separating what the data shows from what I would investigate further.

I also need more practice answering unexpected follow-up questions without taking too long to organise my response.

## Practice Plan

Over the next two weeks, I will practise interview questions for at least 20 minutes three times per week.

I will focus on five areas:

1. Practising short explanations of SQL joins, data cleaning, descriptive statistics and statistical testing.
2. Rehearsing my two STAR stories until I can explain each one clearly in about two minutes.
3. Practising a short explanation of my PesaPap capstone covering the business problem, tools, findings and recommendations.
4. Reviewing common Junior Data Analyst questions involving Python, SQL, spreadsheets and data visualisation.
5. Completing at least one additional mock interview and asking for feedback on clarity, confidence and technical accuracy.

My goal is to give clear and structured answers while showing both technical ability and business understanding.
